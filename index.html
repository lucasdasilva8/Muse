The gap

Artists need capital. Fans already spend. Neither side gets a fair structure.

Underground and independent artists often need money upfront to keep creating and building momentum. Fans already support them — merch, tickets, streams — but usually with no path to a return.

Muse turns that support into investment: artists offer a defined share of future earnings; fans buy in at terms they can read before they pay.

How a Muse share works

You set the split. We structure the price.

Artists choose how much of defined future earnings to offer, for how long, and at what raise. Muse verifies inputs and maps that into a coherent listing.

01

Artist opens an offer

Raise amount, revenue share %, term, and optional return cap — plus which income categories are included.

02

Muse verifies & prices

Traction (like monthly listeners), disclosed revenue, and financial documents inform a suggested raise and risk label.

03

Fans fund the raise

Each fan owns a fraction of the fan pool equal to what they put in versus the total raise.

04

Payouts when revenue hits

Defined net earnings are shared per the terms until the term ends or the cap is reached.
