Artists

Raise without turning fans into donors

Offer a defined slice of future earnings. Keep control of how much you sell, for how long, and at what raise — with Muse verifying the story behind the price.

What you control

Your offer, your split

  • Raise (R) How much capital you need for studio, tour, marketing, or momentum.
  • Share (S) What % of defined net music income the fan pool receives.
  • Term (T) & cap (C) How long the share lasts, and an optional ceiling on total paid back.
  • Categories Which income counts — typically streaming first; merch/sync optional when verifiable.

What Muse does

Structure and trust

We review identity, traction, and documents. Then we suggest a raise range that fits your terms and verified inputs — so fans can see what they’re buying into.

You can adjust within guardrails. Extreme raises against thin revenue get blocked or need manual review.

Early pilots

Start small, stay real

We’re onboarding a limited set of artists — including campus and independent acts who need funding to commit to their craft.
