Trust

Verification is the product

Without identity checks, document-backed revenue, and clear terms, Muse would just be sending money to a friend. We make the process structured.

01

Identity

Confirm the person listing is who they say they are before any public raise.

02

Traction

Monthly listeners, growth, and release activity — spot-checked against public sources.

03

Financial documents

Distributor statements and related docs back disclosed revenue. Self-reported-only listings stay private or high-risk.

04

Pricing coherence

Raise size must make sense vs adjusted revenue, share %, term, and cap — not unlimited capital on thin numbers.

05

Escrow & payout trail

Funds and distributions are tracked. Fees are disclosed on the listing. (Live rails come after legal review.)

Revenue quality

How we label verification

Self-reported Numbers only — usually not enough for a public listing
Document-backed Statements align with disclosure — standard for pilots
Linked Future: recurring feeds from distributors or banking — highest quality
Verification reduces fraud risk; it does not eliminate artistic or market risk. Past revenue does not guarantee future payouts.
