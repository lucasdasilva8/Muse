About

Born at Brown

Talented musicians deserve better funding options. Fans deserve to be more than listeners who only spend.

Muse started with conversations between artists and fans about what makes revenue reporting trustworthy, what terms feel fair, and what should drive the price of a share.

On campus and beyond, independent artists put real time into their work but can’t always fund the next stage — recording, promotion, touring — so momentum stalls. Fans already care; they just lack a structured way to invest.

We’re building Muse to close that gap: verified traction, document-backed earnings, artist-controlled terms, and payouts tied to defined success — not donations dressed up as investment.

Right now we’re in early access: planning, pilots, and a public site that explains the model honestly. No fake marketplace metrics. No live invest button until the legal path is clear.
