Support that can come back

Back artists you think are underappreciated — with transparent terms, verified inputs, and payouts tied to defined earnings.

What you’re buying

A claim on a revenue share — not company equity

When you invest amount a into a raise R, you own a / R of the fan pool. The pool receives share S of the artist’s defined net for term T (often with a cap).

There is no secondary market in the early product. Your capital is committed for the term. Returns depend on real revenue.

Investments in music revenue are speculative. You can lose money. Muse is not currently offering securities on this site.

Before you pay

Every listing should show

  • Exact terms Share %, term, cap, and included income categories.
  • Verification level Self-reported vs document-backed revenue.
  • Traction summary Listener and growth context Muse reviewed.
  • Fees & risk What Muse charges and that returns are not guaranteed.
